````carousel
### Slide 1: Secure JWT Authentication
![Authentication Screen](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/auth_1781748102367.png)

* **Features**: Outlined with Outfit typography, modern dark background, and interactive fields. Includes a 'Demo Accounts' shortcut panel to pre-fill Admin, Pharmacist, or Manager credentials instantly.

### Slide 2: BI Analytics Dashboard
![Dashboard Screen](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/dashboard_1781748135091.png)

* **Features**: Live glassmorphic cards showing Total Medicines, Stock Valuation, Accumulated POS Revenue, and Scikit-Learn R² forecast metrics. Charts display Daily Revenue Trend (Recharts Area), Stock Level by Category (Pie Chart), and Top Selling Products (Bar Chart).
<!-- slide -->
### Slide 3: Inventory Master Registry
![Inventory Screen](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/inventory_1781748333223.png)

* **Features**: Comprehensive stock grid showing available vs. reorder levels, cost/retail prices, manufacturer info, and batch numbers. Features dynamic search and filtering by Category, Stock status (Low/Critical), and Supplier.
<!-- slide -->
### Slide 4: Point of Sale (POS) Sales recorder
![Sales Screen](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c950d5/sales_1781748636739.png)

* **Features**: POS screen allowing pharmacists to record client transactions. Performs real-time inventory validation (prevents over-selling) and calculates total checkout amounts. Logs transactions with a timestamp and handles immediate Firestore stock updates.
<!-- slide -->
### Slide 5: AI Demand Forecasting
![Forecast Screen](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/forecast_1781748732534.png)

* **Features**: Generates a daily 30-day demand forecast for any selected product. Displays predictions, calculated safety stocks, and dynamic reorder recommendations alongside ML metrics comparison charts.
<!-- slide -->
### Slide 6: Alert Center Dashboard
![Alert Center Screen](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/alert_center_page_1781748786915.png)

* **Features**: Consolidated view of critical risk vectors. Aggregates Low/Critical stocks, Near Expiry alerts, and Overstock warnings, and maps them to actionable procurement reorder suggestions.
<!-- slide -->
### Slide 7: Expiry Warnings & Restocking
![Maximized Alert](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/alert_maximized_1781749037042.png)

* **Features**: Closer look at the near-expiry alert cards, indicating remaining days and dates to prevent sales of out-of-date batches.
<!-- slide -->
### Slide 8: Real-Time Risk Feeds
![Alert Notifications](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/alerts_1781749233663.png)

* **Features**: Actionable risk center feed. Products requiring procurement list active restock links to trigger dynamic purchase orders.
<!-- slide -->
### Slide 9: Restocking Priority List
![Restocking List](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/alert_center_left_1781749961278.png)

* **Features**: List view focused on restocking suggestions, mapping target items directly to their supplier designations.
<!-- slide -->
### Slide 10: Procurement Purchase Order Modal
![Procurement Modal](C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/a34d9e91-77ee-48aa-9265-adc8e8c900d5/po_modal_details_1781750045806.png)

* **Features**: Interactive dialog to place purchase orders to specific suppliers (e.g. Alembic, GSK) to restore stock levels. Confirming updates inventory records dynamically.
````
